# ROOT Connection Center: Source Verification Record

**Scope:** Mendocino County public pathways in the ROOT Connection Center.  
**Reviewed:** 2026-08-12 UTC  
**Publication boundary:** ROOT is an independent private planning tool. It has **no provider-referral agreement**, does not submit forms, does not determine eligibility or availability, and does not send member information to any source listed here.

## Published Pathways

| ROOT pathway | Official source basis | What ROOT may accurately state | What ROOT must not state |
|---|---|---|---|
| Housing assistance | Community Development Commission of Mendocino County, listed by the Mendocino County Library as a housing-assistance resource. [1] | The source is an official public housing-assistance entry point. | That housing is available, that a person is eligible, or that ROOT can submit an application. |
| Homelessness coordination | Mendocino County Homeless Services Continuum of Care. [2] | The county describes a coordinated approach and directs people to Continuum of Care resources. | That ROOT operates coordinated entry, shelter, case management, or intake. |
| Food navigation | Mendocino County Library Community Resources. [1] | The county library lists food distribution and community-resource pathways. | That a listed food provider has current inventory, a slot, or a relationship with ROOT. |
| Benefits | Mendocino County Employment & Family Assistance Services. [3] | The county provides official routes for CalWORKs, CalFresh, Medi-Cal, CMSP, EBT support, and related assistance. | That benefits will be approved, expedited, or retained; ROOT does not file or transmit an application. |
| Work | Mendocino County CalWORKs Job Services. [4] | The county describes employment and training services for TANF recipients. | That ROOT guarantees work, training admission, or a provider relationship. |
| Broad resource navigation | 211 Mendocino, also referenced in county-library community resources. [1] [5] | It is an official local information-and-referral directory across multiple categories. | That every listing is current or that 211 has received member data from ROOT. |
| Behavioral-health access | Mendocino County Behavioral Health & Recovery Services. [6] | The county publishes access and language-assistance information. | That ROOT delivers clinical services, confirms coverage, or makes a treatment recommendation. |
| Non-crisis emotional support | Mendocino County Warm Line information published by Behavioral Health & Recovery Services. [6] | The county identifies the Warm Line as non-crisis emotional support, with its listed schedule and contacts. | That it is emergency dispatch, 24/7, or a substitute for emergency response. |
| Urgent mental-health crisis support | Mendocino County Mental Health information. [7] | The county lists a 24/7 mental-health crisis line. | That ROOT is a crisis provider, dispatcher, clinical service, or emergency substitute. |

## Member Data Boundary

The catalog accepts no provider forms and transmits no provider-facing referral packet. A signed-in ROOT member can save only a source identifier and a self-selected local progress state—**saved**, **ready to act**, or **my step is complete**—within ROOT’s encrypted account store. The record intentionally excludes free-text case notes, address, location, household information, health information, eligibility assertions, and provider message content.

Deleting the member account removes the corresponding private action-plan records from ROOT. If the member independently uses an official source, that external interaction is outside ROOT’s control and cannot be revoked by deleting a ROOT record.

## Source Review Policy

The Directory Steward may review Mendocino County public-source accuracy and remove or revise a card. The steward cannot view member action plans, cannot contact a provider from an issue report, cannot publish Lake County sources, and cannot create a provider relationship. Lake County remains internal watchlist-only until a separately authorized publication scope exists.
