# Lake County Publication and Offline Geospatial Source Record

**Scope authorized by the user:** Lake County is now part of ROOT’s published regional scope, alongside Mendocino County.  
**Reviewed:** 2026-08-13 UTC  
**Publication boundary:** ROOT presents official public pathways only. It does not submit applications, determine eligibility, dispatch services, establish a provider relationship, or transmit member data.

## Lake County Public Pathways

| ROOT pathway | Official source basis | Published boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Housing | Lake County’s MHSA Housing Program information describes the program’s stated target population and links the Lake County Continuum of Care. [1] | ROOT does not claim housing availability or eligibility. |
| Benefits and employment | Lake County Social Services publishes benefit, medical-program, social-service, and employment-service information. [2] | ROOT does not submit an application or retain application data. |
| Community navigation | 211 Lake County provides category-based resource navigation and 24/7 contact options. [3] | 211 listings and availability remain with 211 and the underlying providers. |
| Behavioral-health access | Lake County Behavioral Health Services publishes behavioral-health information and a 24-hour member access line. [4] | ROOT does not provide clinical care or determine coverage. |
| Urgent mental-health crisis support | Lake County Behavioral Health Services publishes a 24-hour emergency crisis line. [4] | ROOT does not dispatch emergency services; 911 remains the path for life-threatening emergencies. |

## Offline Orientation Map

The public offline map contains generalized Mendocino and Lake County boundary polygons sourced from the California Department of Technology’s public county-boundary feature layer. The state describes the layer as the best available statewide source but warns that it is not a legal boundary or parcel-location tool. [5] ROOT preserves that limitation: the pack is for regional orientation only, not parcel, legal, property, or site-level determination.

Lake County’s GIS portal itself identifies public maps, wildfire-history material, hazard-related material, and data-download resources, while warning that displayed information is advisory and not a legal parcel boundary. [6] ROOT therefore does not ingest parcels, owner names, private addresses, member location, assessment sites, project locations, or operational work features into its public map.
